Calendar Club

Everything you need to get signed in and set up. Takes about two minutes.

The two apps

Calendar Clubhttps://BFTeutenberg.github.io/calendar-club/ Contactshttps://shared-contacts.github.io/Contacts/

Your email address and password are sent to you separately. The same pair works on both apps. You don't need to create an account — one has been made for you.

1. Sign in

Open the Calendar Club address above, type in your email and password, and tap Sign in. You'll land on the stores list for the current season.

If it says your address isn't on the access list, your login is fine but you haven't been added yet. Message Barry and he'll do it in seconds.

2. Install it

Put it on your home screen

  1. With the app open in Safari, tap the share button at the bottom of the screen — the square with an arrow coming out of the top.
  2. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top right.

You'll get an icon on your home screen. Tapping it opens the app full screen with no address bar, like any other app.

This only works in Safari. If you opened the link in Chrome you won't see the option — copy the address into Safari first.

Put it on your home screen

  1. With the app open in Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen if that's what yours says.
  3. Confirm.

You'll get an icon in your app drawer and on your home screen. It opens full screen with no address bar.

Put it on your desktop

  1. With the app open in Chrome or Edge, look at the right-hand end of the address bar for the install icon — a small screen with a downward arrow in Chrome, or a screen with a plus in Edge.
  2. Click it, then click Install.

If you can't see the icon, use the menu instead. In Chrome that's the three dots → Cast, save and share → Install page as app. In Edge it's the three dots → Apps → Install this site as an app.

It opens in its own window with no address bar and appears in your Start menu. Right-click it there to Pin to taskbar if you want it permanently to hand.

Put it in your dock

In Chrome or Edge: look at the right-hand end of the address bar for the install icon — a small screen with a downward arrow. Click it, then Install.

In Safari: go to File → Add to Dock, then click Add.

Either way it opens in its own window with no address bar. Drag it from Applications or Launchpad into the dock to keep it handy.

3. Do the same for Contacts

Open the Contacts address and repeat both steps. You end up with two separate icons, one for each app.

Finding your way round

There's a Help tab along the top of Calendar Club covering searching, what the colours and dates on a store mean, how the figures work and how to add things. Worth two minutes when you first sign in.

A few things that catch people out: the app opens on the current season, so change the year dropdown to see earlier ones. The area dropdown narrows everything to one TM's stores and remembers your choice. Search matches people as well as stores, so a builder's name finds every store they've worked on. And you can tap an operator's phone number to ring them straight from the store screen.

If something isn't working

Nothing appears after signing in — your email hasn't been added to the access list yet. Ask Barry.

You can see things but can't change them — your access is view only, which is deliberate for most people. Ask Barry if you need more.

It looks out of date or behaves oddly — sign out and back in.
